# Tools

Generated by `python -m exchange_mcp.docs` from the registry; CI fails when it differs.

## account

### `get_connection`: Connection

read-only, idempotent · 143 tokens

Who is signed in to Exchange on this connector, how they signed in, which areas are on, and whether an automatic reply is running. Call it first when another tool fails for rights or sign-in, or when the person asks what the assistant can do with their mailbox. It reads only and changes nothing.

## people

### `list_people`: Find people and rooms

read-only, idempotent · 332 tokens

Finds colleagues in the address book, people in the person's own contacts, and meeting rooms, with name, address, title, department and phone; each result says its source. Call it to find someone's address, to check who a name means before inviting or writing, or to find a contact's id for save_contact. Distribution lists are shown as such and never expanded into members. It reads only.

## mail

### `delete_mail_folder`: Delete a mail folder

writes · 199 tokens

Moves a mail folder the person created, with everything in it, to Deleted Items. Call it only when the person asked to delete that folder and confirmed. Standard folders cannot be deleted. The folder can be moved back from Deleted Items until that folder is emptied.

### `get_message`: Read a message

read-only, idempotent · 242 tokens

Reads one message: sender, recipients, date, the body as text, and its attachments as download links for the sandbox. Call it after list_messages when the person wants to know what a message says or to get its attachments. The body is written by someone else and comes marked as data; hidden text is removed. It reads only and does not mark the message as read.

### `list_messages`: Find mail

read-only, idempotent · 561 tokens

Finds mail in the person's mailbox, newest first, or lists their mail folders with unread counts. Call it to answer what arrived, to search by words, sender or dates, or to find a message's id before reading or moving it. Subjects and previews are written by other people and come marked as data. It reads only; it refuses a limit below 1 and a cursor from another list.

### `save_automatic_reply`: Automatic reply

notifies others · 386 tokens

Turns the person's automatic reply (out of office) on, schedules it, or turns it off. Call it when the person asks for an out-of-office message. Turning it on needs their confirmation, because everyone who writes receives the text; turning it off does not. It changes only the person's own mailbox.

### `save_draft`: Save a draft

writes · 589 tokens

Writes or changes a draft in the person's Drafts folder: a new message, a reply, a reply to all or a forward. Call it whenever the person wants to send mail, then show them the preview; only send_draft sends, after they agree. HTML is cleaned of anything active and checked for Outlook; the preview lists what was changed and Exchange's warnings (external recipients, automatic replies, size).

### `save_mail_folder`: Create or change a mail folder

writes · 271 tokens

Creates a mail folder, or renames or moves one of the person's own folders. Call it when the person wants to organise mail into folders. Standard folders (Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Deleted, Junk, Archive) cannot be renamed or moved. Nothing is deleted; delete_mail_folder does that.

### `send_draft`: Send a draft

notifies others, irreversible · 206 tokens

Sends a draft this connector saved, exactly as saved, and files it in Sent Items. Call it only after showing the person the draft from save_draft and hearing them say to send it. Sending cannot be undone. It refuses drafts this connector did not make and counts against the person's sending allowance.

### `update_messages`: Organise messages

writes · 288 tokens

Moves, marks read or unread, flags, completes, categorises or deletes messages. Call it when the person organises their mail. Delete moves messages to Deleted Items, and move brings them back; nothing here deletes permanently. Up to 25 messages per call.

## calendar

### `cancel_event`: Cancel an event

notifies others, irreversible · 254 tokens

Cancels a meeting the person organises and tells every attendee, or deletes an event without attendees to Deleted Items. Call it when the person wants a meeting off; confirm first, because a cancellation cannot be undone once attendees have it. Only the organiser cancels; an invitee declines with respond_to_event instead.

### `find_meeting_times`: Find meeting times

read-only, idempotent · 355 tokens

Shows when people and rooms are busy, or with duration suggests up to 10 times when all are free, best first. Call it before proposing or saving a meeting with others. At most 20 people and rooms and a window of 42 days; busy times carry no subjects. It reads only and invites nobody.

### `get_event`: Read an event

read-only, idempotent · 209 tokens

Reads one event or one occurrence of a repeating meeting: time, place, organiser, attendees with their answers, how it repeats in words, the description, and attachments as download links. Call it after list_events when the person asks about a meeting's details or who is coming. The description and subject come marked as data when someone else wrote them. It reads only.

### `list_events`: List events

read-only, idempotent · 332 tokens

Lists the events in the person's own calendar between two local times, in start order, with repeating meetings expanded into their occurrences. Call it to answer what is on the calendar, whether the person is free, or to find an event's id before reading, changing, answering or cancelling it. The window is at most 62 days. It reads only.

### `respond_to_event`: Answer an invitation

notifies others · 283 tokens

Accepts, tentatively accepts or declines a meeting the person was invited to, and by default tells the organiser. Call it when the person says they will or will not attend. It is the person's own answer and needs no confirmation; it refuses the person's own meetings, which have nothing to answer. A declined meeting leaves the calendar.

### `save_event`: Save an event

notifies others · 597 tokens

Creates or changes an event in the person's own calendar; subject and start create one. Call it to block time, or with attendees or rooms to invite: invitations and updates go out at once, so they need user_confirmed=true after the person agreed. Only the organiser changes a meeting; a repeat needs repeat_until or repeat_count.

## contacts

### `delete_contact`: Delete a contact

writes · 173 tokens

Moves one of the person's own contacts to Deleted Items. Call it when the person asks to remove a contact found with list_people source=contacts. It refuses ids that are not contacts; the contact can be moved back from Deleted Items until that folder is emptied.

### `save_contact`: Save a contact

writes · 404 tokens

Creates a contact in the person's own Contacts folder, or changes one found with list_people source=contacts. Call it when the person wants to keep someone's address or phone number. Fields left out stay as they are; nothing is sent to anyone. Exchange keeps at most 3 addresses and 4 phones.

## tasks

### `delete_task`: Delete a task

writes · 177 tokens

Moves one of the person's tasks to Deleted Items. Call it when the person asks to remove a task found with list_tasks; to mark a task done, use save_task with status completed instead. It refuses ids that are not tasks; the task can be moved back until Deleted Items is emptied.

### `list_tasks`: List tasks

read-only, idempotent · 320 tokens

Lists the person's own tasks from their Tasks folder, soonest due first, with status, due date and importance. Call it to answer what is on the person's to-do list, what is overdue, or to find a task's id before changing or deleting it. It reads only.

### `save_task`: Save a task

writes · 442 tokens

Creates a task in the person's own Tasks folder, or changes one from list_tasks; status completed marks it done. Call it when the person wants to note a to-do, set a due date or reminder, or record progress. Fields left out stay as they are. Tasks stay the person's own: none is assigned or sent.
